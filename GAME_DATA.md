# Level Data
A few things to point out:
- The numbers in parentheses point out the changes from the normal build. Starting speed and base stop time will not be pointed out as the formulas for calculating rise speed and stop time have been changed.
- **Base Stop Time:** Refers to the amount of stop time a player gets when they make a +4 and they have no garbage queued, and the the stack is not in danger.
- **Garbage Margin:** When a player has accumulated the number of garbage pieces, then the player will have 0 stop time.
- **Shock Frequency:** The number of panels a player has to clear to earn a shock panel.
- **Shock Cap:** The maximum amount of shock panels a player can have queued up.
