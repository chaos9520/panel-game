# Challenge Mode
Challenge Mode has been revamped again from version 1.1. The naming scheme has been changed
## How Damage Works
The number of lines a garbage block would send is equal to the amount of damage it does. All +4s do damage on this build, and chain garbage is capped at 12 damage. Shocks do 1.5 lines of damage.
## The Challenges
### Skill Test
This challenge is designed to help players that are new to this build gauge what skill level they are around. 4 stages, played on levels 2, 4, 6, and 8.
### Normal Challenges
All of the difficulties are divided into versions, and the difficulty level dictates what levels are used.
- v1: The normal difficulty.
- v2: Harder than v1.
- v3: Even harder than v2.
~~--------------------~~
- Very Easy: Levels 1 and 2
- Easy: Levels 3 and 4
- Normal: Levels 5 and 6
- Hard: Levels 7, 8, and 9
- Very Hard: Levels 10, 11, and 12
### Line Clears
You have 2 minutes to send the required amount of damage in order to clear each stage.
- Easy: Levels 3 and 4, 4 stages
- Normal: Levels 5 and 6, 6 stages
- Hard: Levels  7 through 9, 6 stages
### Extras
I have added some extra difficulties that showcase the added game modes and provide extra challenges. Fair warning: they're very challenging!
- Gauntlet: Features the Classic game mode. A real test of endurance. Levels 7 through 9.
- Chaos Mode: Features the Chaos game mode. Levels 7 through 9.
- Hard Mode: Features the Hard game mode. Levels 7 through 9.
- Insane Mode: Features the Insane game mode. Levels 7 through 9.
- Ridiculous Mode: ..let's just say, the garbage patterns area bit... different. :joy: Level 8.
- Panel Hell: JAIL. Life sentence. No parole. :smiling_imp: Levels 10 through 13.
