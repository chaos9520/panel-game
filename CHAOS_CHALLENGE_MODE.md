# Challenge Mode
Challenge Mode has been revamped again. They are very different from the vanilla build, so take a look below.
# How Damage Works
The number of lines a garbage block would send is equal to the amount of damage it does. All +4s do damage on this build, and chain garbage is capped at 12 damage. Shocks do 1.5 lines of damage.
# The Challenges
All the challenges have 12 stages except for Impossible, which has 8 stages. The number listed before the difficulty name is the level it is played on.
## The Basic Challenges
These challenges are designed to help players learn the fundamentals. Players will not see unconventional garbage, and stage difficulty is ordered by GPM (lowest first).
1. Beginner - 4 colors. For new players.
2. Super Easy - 5 colors.
3. Very Easy - 5 colors.
4. Easy - 5 colors.
## The Tough Challenges
You will start to see unconventional garbage in certain stages, as if Chaos Mode was turned on against you. The patterns in these challenges are meant to simulate gameplay against various players. Fair warning: there is a noticeable spike in difficulty going from Easy to Normal, which is by design. Good luck!
5. Normal - 5 colors.
6. Hard - 5 and 6 colors.
7. Very Hard - 6 colors.
8. Super Hard - 6 and 7 colors.
9. Intense - 4 through 7 colors.
10. Insane - 4 through 7 colors.
11. Impossible - 4 through 7 colors.

Note: Normal and harder is where unconventional garbage will start showing up.

Here are my recommendations on where to start, based on perceived skill level:
- If you are new to Panel Attack, then start from **Beginner.**
- If you have some experience playing Tetris Attack or Pokemon Puzzle league, *but cannot beat the hardest difficulties from either game,* then start from **Super Easy** or **Very Easy.**
- If you can beat the hardest difficulties from Tetris Attack or Pokemon Puzzle League, but have never played Panel Attack against other players, then start at **Easy.**
- Silver players coming from the vanilla build should start at **Easy** or  **Normal.**
- Gold and Platinum players coming from the vanilla build should start at **Hard.**
- Diamond and higher-ranked players coming from the vanilla build should start at **Very Hard.**
## Line Clears
You have 2 minutes to send the required amount of damage in order to clear each stage.

- Easy: 4 stages. Level 3, 5 and 6 colors.
- Normal: 6 stages. Level 5, 5 and 6 colors.
- Hard: 6 stages. Level 7, 5 through 7 colors.
- Very Hard: 6 stages. Level 9, 5 through 7 colors.
