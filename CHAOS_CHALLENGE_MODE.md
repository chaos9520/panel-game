## Challenge Mode Difficulties
### Speedrun Friendly
- Easy: This one is for beginners. 8 stages, played on levels 1 and 2.
- Normal: This one is for Novice players. 10 stages, played on levels 3 and 4.
- Hard: This one is for Intermediate players. 12 stages, played on levels 5 and 6.
- Very Hard: This one is also for Intermediate players, but it is more difficult. 12 stages, played on levels 5 and 6.
- Intense: This one is for Advanced players. 12 stages, played on levels 7 and 8.
- Insane: This one is for Expert players. 12 stages, played on levels 9 and 10.
- Impossible: This one is for the Master players. 8 stages, played on levels 10 and 11.
### Endurance Tests
These are far more difficult relative to the equivalent skill level.
- Beginner Gauntlet: Levels 1 and 2
- Novice Gauntlet: Levels 3 and 4
- Intermediate Gauntlet: Levels 5 and 6
- Advanced Gauntlet: Levels 7 and 8
- Expert Gauntlet: Levels 9 and 10
- Master Gauntlet: Levels 10 and 11
### Line Clears
This is something new. You have 2 minutes to send the required amount lines in order to clear each stage. Stage 1 starts at 10 lines, and it increases by 10 each stage.
- Easy: Level 3, 4 stages
- Normal: Level 5, 6 stages
- Hard: Level 8, 8 stages
- Chaos Mode: Level 11, 8 stages
