# Challenge Mode
Challenge Mode has been revamped again from version 1.1. The naming scheme has been changed
## How Damage Works
The number of lines a garbage block would send is equal to the amount of damage it does. All +4s do damage on this build, and chain garbage is capped at 12 damage. Shocks do 1.5 lines of damage.
## The Challenges
### Chaos League Challenges
The Chaos League challenges use the Modern game mode and Modern queue style.
- Beginner: Levels 1 and 2
- Novice: Levels 3 and 4
- Intermediate: Levels 5 and 6
- Advanced: Levels 7 and 8
- Expert: Levels 8 and 9
- Master: Levels 10 thru 12
### The Meijin Series Challenges
The Meijin Series challenges use the Classic game mode and Classic queue style.
- Bronze: Level 5
- Silver: Level 6
- Gold: Level 7
- Platinum, Diamond, and Master: Level 8
- Title Match: Levels 8 and 9

### Line Clears
You have 2 minutes to send the required amount of damage in order to clear each stage.
- Easy: Levels 3 and 4, 4 stages
- Normal: Levels 5 and 6, 6 stages
- Hard: Levels 7 through 9, 6 stages
- Very Hard: Levels 10 through 12, 6 stages
