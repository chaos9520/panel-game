# Level Data
A few things to point out:
- **Base Stop Time:** Refers to the amount of stop time a player gets when they make a +4 and they have no garbage queued, and the the stack is not in danger.
- **Garbage Margin:** When a player has accumulated the number of garbage pieces, then the player will have 0 stop time.
- **Shock Frequency:** A player has to clear the number of panels to earn a shock panel.

## Classic Style Levels
The only differences between these levels and their counterparts on the Modern styles are the following:
- These levels have 0 stop time.
- These levels have drastically increased health.
- Shocks will not spawn, as these levels are playable in 1p Endless and Time Attack only.
