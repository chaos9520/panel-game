# Level Data
There are a few things to point out:
- Base Stop Time: The amount of stop time a player gets when they make a +4 and they have no garbage queued, and the the stack is not in danger.
- Garbage Margin: When a player has accumulated or exceeded the amount in garbage pieces, then the player will have 0 stop time.
- Shock Frequency: The number of panels a player has to clear to earn a shock panel.
- Shock Cap: The maximum amount of shock panels a player can have queued up.
## The Levels by Skill
### Beginner
The Beginner levels are for players that are new to Panel Attack and have never played similar games from a certain franchise.
- Level 1 has 4 colors, and Level 2 has 5 colors.
- Players with a rating of 799 or lower on this build are considered Beginner players.
### Novice
The Novice levels are for players that have some experience playing Panel Attack or similar franchise games, but are still learning basic skills.
- Level 3 has 5 colors, and Level 4 has 6 colors.
- Players with a rating between 800 and 1199 on this build are considered Novice players.
### Intermediate
The Intermediate levels are the perfect starting point for players that have no problem completing the hardest Vs. Com levels from the franchise games, but have limited experience playing against other players.
- Level 5 has 5 colors, and Level 6 has 6 colors.
- Players with a rating between 1200 and 1599 on this build are considered Intermediate players.
### Advanced
***The Advanced levels are the 'gold standard' of competitive play.***
- Level 7 has 5 colors, Level 8 has 6 colors, and level 9 has 7 colors.
- Players with a rating between 1600 and 1999 on this build are considered Advanced players.
### Expert
The Expert levels provide an even greater challenge compared to the Advanced levels.
- Level 9 has 5 colors, and Level 10 has 6 colors.
- Players with a rating between 2000 and 2399 on this build are considered Expert players.
### Master
Level 11 is the pinnacle of top-tier gameplay.
- Level 11 is similar to level 10, but has 7 colors instead of 6.
- Players with a rating of 2400 or higher on this build are considered Master players.
